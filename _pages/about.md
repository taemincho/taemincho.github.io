I lead the AI/ML team at BandLab Technologies, building AI-powered tools for music creation. My work spans music generation, audio processing, source separation, voice-to-MIDI, and music information retrieval, from research and model development to real-time deployment on mobile and web, for BandLab's 200M+ creators.

I have a Ph.D. in Music Technology from **[NYU](https://steinhardt.nyu.edu/marl)**, where my thesis focused on chord recognition, back when that meant HMMs. I also have a guitar degree from **[Berklee](https://college.berklee.edu/)**.

Real-time audio and DSP have been a thread throughout my career. I led the development of **[Soundcamp](https://news.samsung.com/global/new-samsung-soundcamp-features-strike-a-chord-with-digital-music-creators)**<a href="https://www.youtube.com/@soundcamp1717" target="_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener" style="margin-left: 0.3em"><i class="fa-brands fa-youtube" style="color: #FF0000; vertical-align: -0.05em"></i></a>, Samsung's mobile DAW, and later worked on BandLab's cross-platform audio engines. When AI moved to the center of music software, I returned to ML, and that's what I do today.

I'm interested in building technology that makes music creation more expressive, accessible, and fun.
